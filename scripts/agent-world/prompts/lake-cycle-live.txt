Create an explorable Earth lake-basin learning landscape about the water cycle. Let a student walk near the shore, see water waves, rising mist above the lake, slowly drifting clouds, and falling rain. These motions should be clearly described as qualitative illustrations, not a validated weather or evaporation simulation. Focus on the lake, surrounding terrain and one distinctive shoreline boulder; omit decorative objects. Keep each moving phenomenon separate from the static scenery. Explain what each effect represents and the limits of the illustration. Preserve realistic physical scale and choose a regional extent compatible with the available terrain generator. This is a preview candidate to inspect before student release.
